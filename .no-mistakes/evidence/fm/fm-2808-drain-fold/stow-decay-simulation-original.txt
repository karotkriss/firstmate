Stow decay policy simulation (opt-in shape)
===========================================
Initial aging entries: 40; admissions: 2 per pass; passes: 60
Usage cohorts: frequent every 5 passes, occasional every 16, never reinforced

Default contract (config/stow-pass-horizon absent)
  Daily cadence:   40 -> 132 entries
  Byte-identical to the pre-change wall-clock policy every pass: True
  No counter is written and no counter is read
  A single unreinforced aging entry survives 29 passes and archives on day 30

Opted in (config/stow-pass-horizon present)
  Daily cadence:   40 -> 82 entries
  Result: the pass horizon fires in the daily-stow home and removes 50 more stale entries

Monthly cadence
  Default:  40 -> 2 entries
  Opted in: 40 -> 2 entries
  Serialized states identical after every pass: True

  The same entry in a default home serializes as:            <!--a:2026-01-01-->
  Opted-in aging remains live through pass 9 and archives on pass 10
  Opted-in perishable remains live through pass 2 and archives on pass 3
  Genuine evidence on pass 9 refreshes the date and clears the counter
  One later no-evidence pass produces counter: 1
