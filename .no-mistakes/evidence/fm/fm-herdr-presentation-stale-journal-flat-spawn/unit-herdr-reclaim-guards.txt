Unit behavior tests (real bin/backends/herdr.sh functions + real jq, fake herdr CLI
serving canned `workspace list` JSON to model workspace presence/absence).
Command: bash tests/fm-backend-herdr.test.sh  (EXIT=0)

  ok - herdr presentation reclaim: a journal whose recorded workspace is gone is
       quarantined and a fresh projection can start
  ok - herdr presentation reclaim: a still-existing, token-matched, unreadable,
       foreign-session, or version 1 journal is left to today's recovery
  ok - herdr presentation rollback: a refused spawn removes its journal only after
       its exact workspace is confirmed gone

The rollback test also asserts the matrix: workspace-present:kept, list-failed:kept,
token-mismatch:kept, confirmed-gone:removed -> the journal is deleted ONLY when the
exact workspace is proven gone AND the journal still carries this spawn's token, and
only the exact task pane is closed through the focus-safe path.
